RNN LANGUAGE MODEL - DISCUSSION RESPONSES

Thomas, COO of HackPressIO - 

"Do you think we should be using LSTM layers or GRU layers in this network?" 

We chose LSTM layers because they excel at capturing long-range dependencies needed for coherent text generation. Our three stacked LSTM layers progressively build linguistic understanding, making them ideal for 1000+ character outputs.


Monika, Senior Developer - 

"Often when we are generating text we see something like this: 'we counter. He stutn co des...' What would you recommend to improve our results?" 

Our text generation was improperly joining tokens without spacing. We fixed this by generating one word at a time and joining with spaces. More training data and tuning temperature parameters (0.5-0.7 for coherence, 0.8-0.9 for variety) also help.


Thomas, COO of HackPressIO - 

"We're really trying to impress our investors with your work here. What would give us the most promise?"  

Three independently trained author models showcase scalability. Live demos with the same prompt across all authors highlight distinctive voice capture, and our 40-45% loss reduction proves the model learns meaningful patterns.


Johnny, Data Science Intern - 

"I'm wondering what your views are on using a teacher forcing strategy compared to a curriculum learning strategy?" 

We use teacher forcing because it enables faster convergence and stable training with large datasets. It's the standard for sequence models replicating specific author voices.


Monika, Senior Developer - 

"Our previous team used logits in the output layer and then used Sparse Categorical Cross Entropy. Are you planning to use that approach?"

Yes. Logits with SparseCategoricalCrossentropy is the standard for next-token prediction and enables temperature-based sampling during generation.


Thomas, COO of HackPressIO - 

"We could improve our model's performance by supplementing with additional text. Which of the following would you recommend?" 

B) Public domain sources only. Since our goal is capturing individual author voice, adding other authors would dilute style. We recommend using complete works from Project Gutenberg (~50 books per author) for consistency and authenticity.
